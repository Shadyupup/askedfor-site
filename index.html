How it works

Couples ask for vendors in local groups every day. Whoever answers first, with something useful, usually gets the booking. AskedFor makes sure that's you.

We watch the groups

Local bride groups, community and recommendation groups, and the city subreddit are checked around the clock. Dozens of groups in each area, and the list keeps growing.

We screen out the noise

Every post is read and judged: is this a couple actually asking for your category, in your area, with a real date? Vendors advertising, used items for sale and out-of-town posts never reach you.

You reply as yourself

You get one email per lead: the post, the link, the date, and one practical note about the request. You decide whether to answer, and you write it in your own words, from your own account.

Who it's for

Small local vendors who are good at the work and don't have hours a day to scroll groups looking for the next client.

What it never does

Group admins and couples can smell automation. AskedFor is built so that everything the couple sees comes from a real person: you.

  • Never posts or comments for you. Every reply is sent by you, from your own account, only if you choose to.
  • Never messages couples on your behalf. We hand you the lead. The conversation is yours.
  • Never sends the same lead to a crowd. Each lead goes to one matching vendor, not a list.
  • Never sells your data. Your email is used for lead alerts and nothing else. Reply “no” at any time and you're removed.
